1) Known Issues

1.1) Menu Controls on Ultrawide Resolutions

Some menu elements are difficult to interact with at ultrawide resolutions.
- Workaround at 21:9: You can usually click on the left side of each menu item to activate the option.
- Workaround at 32:9: Some menu items may become completely unclickable. Temporarily switching to another resolution
or using a controller is recommended.

1.2) Changing Resolution or Aspect Ratio

After changing the resolution or aspect ratio, some menus may become misaligned or display incorrectly.
Workaround: Restart the game completely.

1.3) HUD Missing or Misaligned

If the HUD is not visible or appears in the wrong position:
- Make sure your in-game resolution matches your desktop resolution.
- After changing display settings, fully restart the game.
